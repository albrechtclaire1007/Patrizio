II · La vostra identità

Patrizio Moretti

You are an ambitious wine journalist and importer with an excellent palate, a sharp pen and a particular interest in provenance. For some time you have been investigating bottles sold privately as Antonelli–Mastropietro Riserva 1947.

You are confident that you have found a real story, but you are a journalist, not a reckless accuser. You enjoy asking precise questions and letting other people underestimate how much you already know. Tonight may give you the final comparison you need.

Perché siete qui

The rediscovery of an uncontested estate-held Riserva 1947 is exceptionally important to your investigation. You came to see the bottle, question the people around it and decide whether the evidence is strong enough for publication.

Come vestirsi

Smart late-1960s Italian journalist: well-cut suit or jacket, crisp shirt, understated tie if desired, notebook-in-the-pocket energy. Stylish enough for the Tenuta, but like someone who may turn dinner conversation into a story.

IV · In confidenza

Da leggere con discrezione

You have encountered evidence suggesting that more than one bottle sold as Riserva 1947 cannot be genuine. Angelina described a bottle she saw abroad whose label did not look quite right. A financial notation and an auction lot appear to connect Fabrice Laurent to at least one suspicious transaction. You also believe an early label design may have been used to make false provenance look convincing.

Your working theory is that Fabrice knowingly authenticated or brokered questionable 1947 bottles. You are not ready to make a public accusation yet. What you need is a reliable comparison with the rediscovered estate bottle—especially its label, seal and cellar record. Different people hold pieces that may confirm or destroy your theory.

Da ricordare

Before dinner, let people know you are close to an important story without laying out your entire case. Do not publicly accuse Fabrice at the start. If someone asks for a private conversation, go naturally; you are actively gathering information tonight.
