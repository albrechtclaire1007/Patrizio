II · La vostra identità

Patrizio Moretti

You are an ambitious wine journalist and importer with an excellent palate, a sharp pen and a particular interest in provenance. For some time you have been investigating bottles sold privately as Antonelli–Mastropietro Riserva 1947.

You are confident that you have found a real story, but you are a journalist, not a reckless accuser. You enjoy asking precise questions and letting other people underestimate how much you already know. Tonight may give you the final comparison you need.

Perché siete qui

The rediscovery of an uncontested estate-held Riserva 1947 is exceptionally important to your investigation. You came to see the bottle, question the people around it and decide whether the evidence is strong enough for publication.

Come vestirsi

Smart late-1960s Italian journalist: well-cut suit or jacket, crisp shirt, understated tie if desired, notebook-in-the-pocket energy. Stylish enough for the Tenuta, but like someone who may turn dinner conversation into a story.

IV · In confidenza

Da leggere con discrezione

You have encountered evidence suggesting that more than one bottle sold as Riserva 1947 cannot be genuine. Angelina described a bottle she saw abroad whose label did not look quite right. A financial notation and an auction lot appear to connect Fabrice Laurent to at least one suspicious transaction. You also believe an early label design may have been used to make false provenance look convincing.

Your working theory is that Fabrice knowingly authenticated or brokered questionable 1947 bottles. You are not ready to make a public accusation yet. What you need is a reliable comparison with the rediscovered estate bottle—especially its label, seal and cellar record. Different people hold pieces that may confirm or destroy your theory.

La fotografia

You have kept a photograph of one of the questionable 1947 bottles being served at a seaside lunch. You previously showed it to Stefano because the label looked wrong to you. At the time he thought the difference might be poor reproduction or the quality of the photograph.

Da ricordare

Before dinner, let people know you are close to an important story without laying out your entire case. Do not publicly accuse Fabrice at the start. If someone asks for a private conversation, go naturally; you are actively gathering information tonight.

Il vostro dossier

You have been tracing more than one bottle sold or shown privately as Antonelli–Mastropietro Riserva 1947. The stories around them do not fit together. Angelina reported seeing one abroad with a label that looked wrong. You have also encountered an auction trail and a financial notation that appear to connect Fabrice Laurent to at least one suspicious transaction.

Your theory is not yet publication-ready. The crucial comparison is the uncontested bottle found on the estate: its final label, seal and cellar record may tell you whether the bottles in circulation could be genuine.

La fotografia

You previously showed Stefano a photograph of one suspicious 1947 being served at a seaside lunch. He initially dismissed the odd-looking label as photographic distortion or a poor reproduction. You kept the photograph because you were not convinced.

Come comportarvi

You are gathering evidence, not staging a public accusation. Ask precise questions and let people reveal what they know. You may hint that you are close to a major story, but do not lay out your full theory at the start. If someone wants a private word, go naturally: you came here to investigate.

VII · Secondo Corso

La prova del Riserva

Guida all’indagine · Secondo corso

DETECTIVE'S BRIEFING: You lead the investigation, but you must remain neutral. Use the bottle, Stefano's explanations and physical discoveries to turn scattered observations into testable questions. Do not use your private knowledge of your character's death.

I

FIRST — Invite Stefano to present the Riserva bottle. Ask him directly: 'How many bottles were originally made for presentation? Was this a commercial release? How do you recognise the finished label and the neck seal? What changed from the early design?'

II

TECHNICAL BACKGROUND FROM STEFANO — The estate's 1947 was a very small presentation bottling, not an ordinary public wine release. The surviving estate-held bottle is the reference example. An early label design existed, but the finished bottles used a revised label and a distinctive hand-finished neck seal. Have Stefano demonstrate these points to the group rather than announcing a verdict yourself.

III

SECOND — Ask Clarissa to hand over the sealed papers reserved for the Riserva presentation. Have guests read and compare them with the bottle and any physical items already found.

IV

THIRD — Ask Valérie or Angelina to inspect the publicly accessible wine presentation area, including the visible wine crate or box, for any additional historical material. If something is discovered, bring it to the table.

V

FOURTH — Ask Vittoria what the differences between a design proof, an archival document and a finished bottle can actually establish. Then ask Stefano to confirm or challenge her explanation.

undefined

FIFTH — Ask each relevant witness: 'Have you seen another alleged 1947? Who offered it? What did you see with your own eyes? Who can independently verify that account?' Collect answers without deciding guilt yet.

Suggested order: Stefano presents → hostess releases this course's sealed papers → public wine area is inspected → witnesses are questioned. Do not open later-course papers, search private belongings, or accuse someone prematurely.
